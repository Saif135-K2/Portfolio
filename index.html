APPROACH

Every campaign starts the same way, regardless of industry:

01
Know the market before touching the message
What are the customer's actual pain points, what does the business offer, and why should anyone trust it over the alternative — answered before any creative is built.
02
Build the message around one hook
A clear tagline and a single emotional trigger — usually urgency or FOMO — tied directly to the pain point just identified, not a generic brand line.
03
Match the content mode to the job it has to do
Graphics keep a platform active and feeding the algorithm day to day — they're maintenance, not the growth driver. Video, especially 9:16 vertical, is what actually moves leads and sales.
